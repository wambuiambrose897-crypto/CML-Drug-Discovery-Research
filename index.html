Drug Discovery Research Study

Comprehensive Analysis: From Target Identification to Preclinical Candidate Selection

Case Study: Chronic Myeloid Leukemia (CML) & BCR-ABL Target

📋 Project Overview

Objective: Investigate the complete drug-discovery pipeline and evaluate how modern pharmaceutical research identifies disease targets, discovers potential drug candidates, evaluates their efficacy and safety, and advances promising compounds toward clinical development.

Total Modules: 10 | Format: Independent Research Reports | Status: Complete

📚 Research Modules

Module 1

Disease Selection & Unmet Medical Need

Investigates why CML is prioritized for drug discovery and what unmet medical needs justify developing new therapeutics.

 Module 2

Target Identification & Validation

Analyzes BCR-ABL as the primary target and compares alternative therapeutic targets with supporting evidence.

 Module 3

Drug Discovery Strategy

Evaluates structure-based design, virtual screening, and computational approaches for BCR-ABL inhibitor discovery.

 Module 4

Hit Identification

Describes methods for identifying initial chemical compounds capable of interacting with the BCR-ABL target.

 Module 5

Hit-to-Lead Optimization

Explains how promising hits are optimized into stronger, more selective lead compounds through medicinal chemistry.

 Module 6

ADME & Pharmacokinetic Evaluation

Assesses absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretion properties critical for clinical development.

 Module 7

Safety & Toxicology Assessment

Examines potential safety risks and includes a case study of a failed drug candidate.

 Module 8

Preclinical Development

Outlines the evidence and testing required before clinical trials, including regulatory preparation.

 Module 9

Real-World Case Study

Analyzes Imatinib's successful development journey and compares it with a failed candidate (TGN1412).

 Module 10

Final Drug-Discovery Proposal

Synthesizes all previous findings into a comprehensive drug-discovery program proposal with timeline and success criteria.
